Logoxo 1 Accessible open-plan loft, old town

  • 49 m²
  • 4 guests
  • Double bed and sofa bed
  • 1 bathroom
  • Ground floor

Logoxo 1 is a loft: 49 m² of completely open space at street level, on the ground floor of a 1900 building in the old town of Zarautz. The sleeping area is part of the living room, with no door between them. The bathroom is separate.

Step-free access

It has its own door onto the pavement: you walk straight in at street level, with no lobby, no stairs and no lift. Doorway widths and the bathroom meet accessibility regulations, so you can move around the whole apartment in a wheelchair or with a walking frame.

The space

A single open room with exposed concrete columns, sleeping up to four.

The street and the neighbourhood

Herrikobarra is a quiet pedestrian street, right in the old town but away from the bustle of Kale Nagusia and Zigordia. The atmosphere is round the corner and the quiet is at home.
