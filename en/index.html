Logoxo

Zarautz

The longest beach in Gipuzkoa, five minutes away

Two and a half kilometres of sand, waves to surf all year round, the seafront promenade for a stroll and an old town full of pintxo bars. All on foot from your door.

Location

The street and the neighbourhood
