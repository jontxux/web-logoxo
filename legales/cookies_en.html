Cookie policy

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser so that it works properly or to remember information about your visit.

2. Cookies used by this website

The pages of this website do not set any cookies, either their own or third-party: there is no advertising, no analytics and no content embedded from other sites. That is why no cookie notice is shown.

Booking and payment take place on Lodgify's booking checkout (checkout.lodgify.com), which you reach by pressing “Book”. That checkout does use cookies:

  • Technical cookies (necessary): essential to complete a booking and exempt from consent (art. 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE).

  • Analytics cookies (only with your consent): Google Analytics, set by Codebay Solutions Limited (Lodgify), which is responsible for them. They are only activated if you accept them in the cookie notice shown by the checkout.

  • Stripe: on the payment page, to process the charge securely and prevent fraud. More information in Stripe's privacy policy.

3. How to manage them

On the booking checkout you can accept, reject or customise analytics cookies from its own cookie notice. You can also allow, block or delete cookies from your browser settings. If you block technical cookies, you may not be able to complete a booking.
